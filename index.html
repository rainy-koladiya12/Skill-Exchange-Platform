How SkillBridge Works

A simple 4-step loop to learn and teach anything

1

List Your Skill

Post what you are good at (coding, drawing, playing guitar, speaking French) with your experience level.

2

Send / Accept Requests

Find a skill you want to learn, choose a convenient date and time, and send a learning request.

3

Meet & Learn

Once accepted, an active session is auto-scheduled. Connect, share knowledge, and level up!

4

Leave a Review

Complete the session and leave transparent star ratings to build your campus reputation.
